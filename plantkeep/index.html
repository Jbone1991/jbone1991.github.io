PlantKeep Support

PlantKeep identifies plants, diagnoses what is wrong with them, tells you when to water, and warns you about dangerous plants on the trail.

Get help

Email jandastoresllc@gmail.com and we will get back to you. Telling us your device and what you were doing when something went wrong helps us fix it faster.

Common questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they are cancelled in Settings on your device: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then PlantKeep. Cancelling stops the renewal and you keep Pro until the period you already paid for runs out.

What happens to my plants if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted or hidden. Only the daily limits on the AI photo features come back. Watering, reminders, the field guide, your journal and export stay free forever.

How do I get my data out?

Settings, then Export my plants. It is free, needs no account, and produces a readable file.

Is the plant safety information reliable?

Treat it as a starting point, never as the final word. PlantKeep never tells you a plant is safe to eat. If someone has eaten an unknown plant in the United States, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.
