PlantKeep Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026
Operator: J&A Stores LLC ("we", "us")

The short version

Your data lives on your phone. We run no analytics, show no ads, and sell nothing about you. The only time data leaves your device is when you use an AI feature (the specific content you submit is processed and not stored) or when you turn on optional cloud backup.

What the app stores

PlantKeep keeps plant photos, your saved plant list, your field journal of wild finds, and any photos queued for identification while you were offline, all on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data unless you enabled cloud backup.

You can export everything to a readable file at any time from Settings → Export my plants. No account is needed, and it is not a paid feature. If a subscription lapses, nothing is deleted.

AI features

We ask before we send anything. The first time you use a photo feature, PlantKeep shows a disclosure naming Anthropic and asks you to allow it. Nothing is sent unless you tap Allow. You can withdraw that permission at any time in Settings → Photo analysis, and the photo features simply stop working.

The photo features are: plant identification, plant health checks, wild plant identification, the "is this dangerous?" safety check, and the soil check. When you use one, the single photo you are checking is sent over an encrypted connection to Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI, to generate the result, subject to their API data policies. Photos are processed to answer your question and are not retained by Anthropic or used to train their models. The result is stored on your device, not on a server.

Your plant list, watering history, care notes, field journal and export file are never sent to Anthropic.

Everything that does not need a photo works without this permission: the offline field guide, watering and reminders, the plant catalogue, daily tips, the light check, adding plants by name, your field journal, and export.

Optional cloud backup

If you sign in and enable backup, your plant list is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) under your account so you can restore it on a new device. You can delete your account and all cloud data at any time from Settings → Delete account & data. Photos stay on-device and are not uploaded.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google and managed through RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or ad SDKs
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No sale or sharing of personal data
  • No bank or account connections

Children

PlantKeep is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
