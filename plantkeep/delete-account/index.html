Delete Your PlantKeep Account

You can delete your PlantKeep account and every piece of cloud data tied to it directly inside the app:

  1. Open PlantKeep.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account & data.
  4. Confirm.

This permanently removes your sign-in account and your cloud backup (your saved plant list) from our servers. It cannot be undone. Plants stored on your device stay on your device; uninstalling the app removes those.

If you signed in recently the deletion completes immediately. If it has been a while, the app may ask you to sign in again first to confirm it's really you.

Can't access the app?

Email jandastoresllc@gmail.com from the address you used to sign in and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm and remove your account and all associated cloud data within 30 days.

What we keep

Nothing. PlantKeep stores no analytics or advertising data, so once your account and backup are deleted there is nothing left to keep.
